# **Mini‑Suite Cheatsheet (for Writers)**

A compact reference for **[mini.ai](https://mini.ai)**, **[mini.surround](https://mini.surround)**, **[mini.comment](https://mini.comment)**, **[mini.pairs](https://mini.pairs)**, and **[mini.indentscope](https://mini.indentscope)** — tuned for prose, clarity, and flow.

---

## **1. [mini.ai](https://mini.ai) — Text Objects**

|Action|Command|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**Select inside sentence**|`vis`|Precise sentence selection|
|**Select around sentence**|`vas`|Includes punctuation|
|**Change inside sentence**|`cis`|Rewrite a sentence cleanly|
|**Delete a sentence**|`das`|Remove entire sentence|
|**Select inside paragraph**|`vip`|Core editing motion|
|**Select around paragraph**|`vap`|Includes blank lines|
|**Change inside paragraph**|`cip`|Rewrite a whole paragraph|
|**Inside quotes**|`vi"` / `vi'`|Select quoted text|
|**Inside parentheses**|`vib`|Select inside `()`|
|**Inside Markdown block**|`vim`|Auto-detected block selection|

---

## **2. [mini.surround](https://mini.surround) — Surround Editing**

|Action|Command|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**Add surround**|`sa"`|Wrap in `"quotes"`|
||`sa'`|Wrap in `'single quotes'`|
||`sa)`|Wrap in `(parentheses)`|
||`sa]`|Wrap in `[brackets]`|
||`sa*`|Wrap in `*italics*`|
||`sa_`|Wrap in `_emphasis_`|
|**Delete surround**|`sd"`|Remove `"quotes"`|
||`sd)`|Remove `(parentheses)`|
|**Replace surround**|`sr"'`|`"double"` → `'single'`|
||`sr)}`|`( )` → `{ }`|
|**Find surround**|`sf"` / `sF"`|Jump to next/previous `"..."`|
|**Highlight surround**|`sh`|Visualize current surrounding|

## **3. [mini.comment](https://mini.comment) — Drafting & Notes**

|Action|Command|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**Toggle comment (line)**|`gcc`|Comment/uncomment line|
|**Comment selection**|`gc` (visual)|Works on any selection|
|**Comment paragraph**|`gc}`|Comment until next blank line|

## **4. [mini.pairs](https://mini.pairs) — Autopairs**

|Action|Behavior|
|---|---|
|Type `(`|Auto‑closes `)`|
|Type `[`|Auto‑closes `]`|
|Type `"`|Auto‑closes `"`|
|Type `*`|Auto‑closes `*` (Markdown emphasis)|

Lightweight, predictable, and prose‑friendly.
